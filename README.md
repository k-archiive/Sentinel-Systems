# Houston Flood-Aware Route Planner

#Problem Statement
Houston frequently experiences heavy rainfall and flash flooding, causing road closures, hazardous driving conditions, and significant disruptions to daily travel. Traditional navigation applications primarily optimize routes based on distance and traffic conditions, often failing to account for real-time flood risks, flooded roadways, and rapidly changing weather events. As a result, commuters, emergency responders, and delivery personnel may unknowingly encounter unsafe routes, leading to delays, property damage, and threats to public safety.

The Houston Flood-Aware Route Planner aims to address this challenge by integrating real-time flood monitoring, weather forecasts, traffic updates, and road closure data to recommend safer travel routes. The system will analyze flood-prone areas and current conditions, calculate alternative paths with lower risk, and provide timely alerts to users. By prioritizing both safety and efficiency, the solution seeks to reduce flood-related travel hazards and improve mobility during extreme weather events.

#Target users
Target users would be Houston residents and commuters
